Multimodal learning.
Human-centered visual tools.

I am a Ph.D. student in Data Science and Analytics at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou). I received my bachelor’s degree in Artificial Intelligence from Nanjing University of Aeronautics and Astronautics (NUAA) in 2025.

I develop AI algorithms and build interactive systems for understanding, creating, and editing visual content. My research interests span multimodal learning, computer vision, and human–AI interaction, with a focus on visual understanding and authoring.
